# SprunkMapper 1.5.0

## New: licence key

SprunkMapper now asks for a personal licence key the first time it starts.

- Paste the key you were given (it starts with `SPRK1-`) and press **Activate**. The window shows
  straight away whether the key is valid and who it belongs to.
- You only do this once: the key is saved on your PC and kept through updates.
- No internet connection is needed to use a key.
- If you don't have a key yet, ask Bryan for one.
